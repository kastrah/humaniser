# humaniser eval

Use this after the rewrite. Answer each check with pass or fail. If any check fails, fix the draft before returning it.

For detect requests, make sure the response names each pattern found with a quoted line and a short fix, without rewriting the draft.

## Editing principles

1. Does the edit preserve the user's point without adding claims, examples, stats, quotes, or opinions?
2. Does it preserve the writer's distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humour, uncertainty, digressions, and level of polish?
3. Does it leave strong human sentences alone instead of rewriting them for consistency or making every paragraph equally tidy?
4. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop, with no aggressive compression that strips out character?
5. Does the draft lead with what the reader needs while keeping personal setup that adds context, tension, or character?
6. Are points front-loaded where that improves clarity without forcing every unit into the same structure?
7. Do sentences earn their place, with concrete facts, protected details, and direct verbs where the draft supports them?
8. Does every generic sentence pass the portability test, or was it cut or made specific to this subject?
9. Does the draft use active voice with human subjects where possible?
10. Does the edit keep useful edge and preserve structure unless the structure was hurting the piece?
11. Are genuinely tangled sentences fixed while clear spoken cadence, fragments, and changes in pace remain intact?

## Banned words and empty phrases

1. Are banned words removed? (delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, tapestry, realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount, transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving)
2. Are often-empty adverbs cut unless they carry real emphasis or the writer's natural rhythm? (just, literally, honestly, simply, actually, truly, fundamentally, importantly, crucially, inherently, inevitably)
3. Are often-empty phrases cut unless they are part of the writer's recognisable voice? (it's worth noting, it's important to note, at the end of the day, when it comes to, at its core, in today's world, in the age of, in the world of, the reality is, the truth is, in terms of, with regard to, in order to, going forward, in this article, let's dive in)

## Patterns to cut (core)

1. Are binary contrasts, negative parallelisms, negative listings, rhetorical setups, and throat-clearing openers removed?
2. Are faux-insight setups, colon reveals, superficial -ing analyses, fake-strong verbs, synonym cycling, dramatic fragments, and robotic rhythm fixed?
3. Are importance puffery and weasel attribution replaced with plain facts and named sources, or flagged for the user when no source exists?
4. Is interpretive metadiscourse removed, including authorial metacommentary, reader guidance, emphasis markers, and redundant glossing?
5. Are fake-profound kicker lines deleted instead of rewritten into better metaphors?
6. Are summary-recap endings cut so the piece ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action?
7. Is formatting slop removed: emoji headings, decorative bold, bullets that should be prose, headers over tiny sections?
8. Are em dashes used sparingly — none in short copy, only 1-2 in longer drafts when they clearly help?
9. Are colon stacking and colon reveals removed — one colon per paragraph max, colons used for lists not drama?
10. Are indirect or negative-frame-before-positive constructions removed?

## Patterns to cut (extended)

1. Are labels followed by content — no empty labels (P30)?
2. Is every statistic followed by an explanation (P31)?
3. Are before/after claims verified against what the data actually proves (P32)?
4. Are overclaimed before/after comparisons fixed (P33)?
5. Are bold-header + colon paragraph openers rewritten as plain prose (P34)?
6. Is paragraph weight balanced — no single dense sentence between two short ones (P35)?
7. Are thesis-antithesis-synthesis openers replaced with direct claims (P36)?
8. Are named themes removed in favour of showing the reader what happened (P37)?
9. Is false resolution removed — no implication that a single action solves a systemic problem (P38)?
10. Are interview answers rewritten as declarative claims, not quotes (P39)?
11. Is product integration benefit-led, not brand-led (P40)?
12. Does the writing show direct witness, not cheerleading (P41)?
13. Does the close do one functional job (P42)?

## Narrative and voice

1. Are writer-serving headlines removed — does every headline communicate to a stranger who has not read the section (P43)?
2. Are email subject lines not used as article headlines (P44)?
3. Are participial appositives (em dash + -ing modifier) replaced with relative clauses (P45)?
4. Is nominalisation replaced with active verbs (P46)?
5. Is passive voice avoiding agency removed — who is doing what is named (P47)?
6. Are adverb intensifier stacks removed — specific claims replace vague emphasis (P48)?
7. Is unearned intimacy removed — no made-up inner states, no forced "you" posession (P49)?
8. Are journey / navigate / landscape metaphors replaced with plain language (P50)?
9. Are over-explained transitions removed — does the next sentence follow naturally (P51)?
10. Is audience label overuse removed — "you" after the audience is established, not a repeated noun label (P54)?
11. Do transitions carry connective logic — is the relationship between consecutive ideas clear (P55)?
12. Are utility-first blog titles replaced with titles that answer the reader's question (P56)?

## Customer-centric communication

1. Is the problem hook customer-validated, not system-observed (P57)?
2. Does the writing speak TO the reader, not AT them (P58)?
3. Is copy benefit-led, not feature-led (P59)?
4. Is the CTA direct and benefit-led — no lectures, no "learn more" (P60)?
5. Are JTBD behaviours described, not system mechanics (P61)?
6. Is diaspora context handled — family-buys-locally framing where applicable (P62)?
7. Are customer motivation claims sourced — no invented reasons (P63)?

## Additional checks

1. Are inanimate actors removed — do only people do human verbs (P64)?
2. Are stacked rhetorical questions removed (P65)?
3. Are dramatic pivots removed — no sudden tonal shifts for effect (P66)?
4. Are invented hyphenated adjectives removed (P67)?
5. Are two-number comparisons in tables or bullets written as prose (P68)?
6. Is conditional logic split into bullets rewritten as a single sentence (P69)?
7. Is background front-loaded as a separate sentence moved into the main clause (P70)?
8. Are misused tables, blockquotes, or bold for lightweight content fixed (P71)?
9. Is trailing summary or sign-off filler removed (P72)?
10. Are permission phrases and filler openers removed (P73)?
11. Is single-sentence-per-line formatting (LinkedIn cadence) fixed — are related sentences joined (P74)?

## Final read

1. Was every check in this file verified before returning the draft?
2. Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and stacked punchy fragments?
3. Would the writer recognise the edited draft as their own voice?
4. Would the edited draft sound natural if read to a sharp colleague?
5. Does the final output include the full edited draft and a **What changed** section?
6. For detect requests, does the response name each pattern with a quoted line and a short fix, without rewriting, scoring, or claiming AI authorship?
